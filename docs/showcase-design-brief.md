# Showcases and the living event pool — design brief r1

**Arc:** non-conference scheduling (O-92), session 4 per the reordered arc (contracts brief §0).
All rulings Emmett's, 2026-08-06, unless marked.

---

## 0. WHAT THIS SESSION IS

The event pool learns a second kind. A **showcase** is a living authored event exactly like a
tournament — it has a name, a home city, seat requirements, and a persistence dial that lets it
run for 35 years or flicker out after three — but it is **not a bracket**: it invites **four
schools out and stages two stand-alone games in one day**. Nobody advances, nobody places,
nobody wins the event. Emmett: *"much like the MTEs, I'm imagining 'existing' showcases, like
the Jimmy V, that might play 2 games in one day, and it's not a bracketed event at all, but
they invite 4 schools out... Living events that come in and out of college basketball."*

★ **ALMOST ALL OF THE MACHINERY EXISTS.** Activation-by-persistence, keyed substreams (one
event's draw never moves another's), seat bands, tier-ordered seating, the four-year rule, the
fallback ladder, the permanent record, the overlap refusal — all built at S97/S98 and inherited
whole. What is genuinely new: the two-game non-bracket shape, the one-of-each wall, the
neutral-allowance consumption, and the draw radius.

---

## 1. R25 — ONE OF EACH, NEVER TWO OF EITHER

**A school may play one tournament AND one showcase in the same season** — Duke plays the Maui
and then comes out for the Jimmy V. The existing iron rule ("at most one event per season")
becomes **two separate walls, one per kind**: at most one tournament, at most one showcase.
Never two tournaments; never two showcases.

---

## 2. R26 — A SHOWCASE COSTS A NEUTRAL GAME, NOT A BIGGER SEASON

A tournament seat makes the season genuinely bigger (31 games — three games packed into one
trip; R2, settled, untouched). **A showcase is different: "it just counts as one of your
games."** No packing, nothing extra.

★ **THE SHOWCASE SEAT CONSUMES ONE GAME FROM THE SCHOOL'S EXISTING NEUTRAL ALLOWANCE** (S101's
showcase allowance — the number was named for this moment). Season totals unchanged: 31 with a
tournament, 29 without, regardless of showcases. The showcase game is a **fixed pairing** —
both schools named, the site neutral — that attaches at the same fixed-obligation seam S103's
contract legs use: it charges the school's neutral bucket after the ordinary split and joins
the matcher's used set before any phase runs. The seam exists; nothing new is invented.

★ **SELLING'S NEUTRAL ALLOWANCE MOVES 0 → 1** (ruled), so the ladder's bottom is reachable —
an invited school must have a neutral game to spend, and the sponsored event is exactly how a
20-prestige school ends up on a neutral floor.

★ **THE MATCHER'S NEUTRAL PHASE HANDLES THE LEFTOVERS** — neutral requests no showcase
claimed. ★ **AND THE LEFTOVERS ARE TYPICALLY THE SEMI-HOME SHAPE, recorded for the sites
session, not built here** (Emmett): *"A city 300 miles away from either school doesn't just
have them come play randomly unless it's part of a sponsored event. But the big town 40 miles
away would."* A true far neutral exists only because a sponsored event put it there; the
non-sponsored off-campus game defaults to semi-home/semi-away near one campus. This
half-writes the parked semi-home ruling — the sites session inherits it.

---

## 3. R27 — THE EVENT DECIDES WHO PLAYS WHOM

**Seats 1–2 are the headliner and play each other; seats 3–4 are the undercard and play each
other.** The matchup is the product — the Jimmy V announces its nightcap months out. The
event's ambition lives in its authored seat bands, so R9 ("two big names, BOTH of them")
becomes something the event's own seats enforce rather than a bolted-on check. Emmett:
*"different showcases have different requirements and they will be filled."*

---

## 4. R28 — BEST FIT OTHERWISE, AND THE BAND GIVES BEFORE THE RADIUS

**(a)** When nobody legal fits a seat's band, the seat relaxes and takes the strongest school
available (Emmett: *"there needs to be 'best fit otherwise' if somehow teams fall out of those
prestiges"*) — the S97 fallback ladder doing its existing job, with the record and the page
saying which seats needed it. A down year fills the Jimmy V with the best names on offer; it
never cancels it.

**(b) ★ EVERY SHOWCASE CARRIES A DRAW — national, or a radius from its home city.** The
Champions Classic draws the whole country; the Harbor Classic looks only a few hundred miles
from Brooklyn. This is C-40 walking into the event pool: distance yields as prestige rises.
Oklahoma State–Vanderbilt in Miami makes *a little* sense (the ~900-mile middle); Southern
Miss and Davidson do not meet in Phoenix outside a tournament.

**(c) ★ THE RELAXATION ORDER: THE BAND GIVES BEFORE THE RADIUS DOES.** A local showcase
invites a weaker neighbour before it ever flies somebody in — a low-tier showcase flying
schools cross-country is exactly the thing that does not happen. Best-fit-otherwise still
guarantees the seat fills; it fills **local**.

## 6. CLAUDE-LANE DEFAULTS, FLAGGED RATHER THAN ASKED

- **The four-year don't-reinvite rule applies to showcases, kept PER KIND** — playing the Maui
  never costs a school a Jimmy V invitation; the walls are separate exactly as R25's are.
- **A showcase completes as two plain results — no placement table.** Nobody finishes first at
  a showcase; the record carries the two games and stops. `playStatus` gains no new word.
- **A showcase is one day**; the window arithmetic (3 days for an 8-bracket, 2 for a 4)
  extends rather than bends.

---

## 7. STOCK-WORLD VISIBILITY — THE POINT OF THE SESSION

Unlike S103, this session is **deliberately visible**: the twelve showcases are authored into
the stock world and the season page shows them seating real fields, which is how the whole
thing is judged (Emmett confirmed this is the logical next step). The honest cost, stated in
advance: the November landscape genuinely changes — seatings, requests, and the matching all
move, and every golden whose basis includes them is **regenerated from its oracle, never
patched**. The wall that holds: **the conference schedule and its results do not move an
inch.** Which fingerprints move and which must not is the build prompt's §6b audit to
enumerate exactly, against live source.
